## 1st Attempt

some important note I need to show you, I'm not using AI to get recipe, I'm using AI to calculate the ingredient, like using overipe banana with or without skin it has different calculation, while I believe 100gr banana equal 20gr sugar


ok the very first thing I did is asking my wife for the recipe, I'm kinda overwhelming with the recipe on the internet, do this and that, so my wife making the banana cake and I watch it on how to make it, after that the banana cake is similiar but too soft, and the banana is not really caramelize and my wife actually using overripe banana the process where the banana will have the overwhelming sugar here.


I don't have any important notes here, but what I believe is I will do this by my self.

so I recreate my wife recipee but I change some, one of the key is I don't use the ribbon stage, its the stage I mix the egg and sugar until I could makes the ribbon over the egg, kinda almost creamy, and this part makes your cake super soft, and I don't want it.

and also I change the part where the first step makes the margarine and sugar, instead I change it into aren sugar.

the result ? 

> better but still soft, I put too much margarine, no banana crunch, no caramelize (FUCK), still smell banana
